From the Founder

Why CHEW exists.

I was not raised with a financial playbook. Nobody sat me down and taught me how credit really worked, how banks looked at borrowers, how businesses got funded, or how ownership could be used to build long-term wealth. A lot of what I know today, I had to teach myself.

The more I learned, the more I realized how much information people are expected to know without anybody ever actually teaching it to them.

I started seeing the same problem everywhere. People could work hard, make money, pay bills, take care of their families — and still have no real understanding of the financial system they were operating inside.

They might know they needed “good credit,” but not understand what lenders were actually looking at. They might want business funding without understanding banking relationships, cash flow, documentation, or underwriting. They might hear one person talk about credit, another about LLCs, another about funding, another about real estate — and nobody showing them how the pieces connected.

Too much of the financial education space was built around shortcuts, hype, and promises instead of teaching people how the system actually works. That bothered me. Because once I started educating myself, I realized this information wasn't impossible to understand.

It was just scattered.

In some cases, the people who needed the information the most were being sold outcomes instead of being taught capability.

So I kept learning — credit, business credit, banking, underwriting, funding, business structure, documentation, cash flow, capital readiness, ownership, real estate, risk. Eventually I stopped seeing these as separate subjects. I started seeing the system underneath all of them.

That's where CHEW comes from.

CHEW means Creating Honest Economic Wealth. The word Honest matters. I didn't want to build a company that tells people what they want to hear just to make a sale. I wanted something that could tell somebody the truth about where they actually stand, teach them what lenders and institutions actually see, and give them the knowledge to make stronger decisions for themselves.

CHEW isn't supposed to create dependence. It's supposed to create understanding. Not shortcuts. Not fake guarantees. Not financial theater. Real education. Real strategy. Real understanding. And eventually, real ownership.

That's also where the creed comes from: We all Chew. You eat. Then you feed the next. Wealth is bigger than one person finally getting money. If I learn something that changes my family's trajectory, and I teach somebody else how to understand it, and they use that knowledge to change another household — the knowledge keeps moving. One person learns. One family becomes stronger. One business gets built correctly. Then that person can help the next one.

I had to self-educate because I did not receive the playbook. CHEW exists because I do not believe the next person should have to figure out every piece alone.

Leroy Thompson

Founder, CHEW LLC
