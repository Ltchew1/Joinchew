How It Works

Ten stages. One long-term relationship.

1

Application

You complete a short assessment and answer honestly. We read every one individually, never sorted by algorithm. You walk away knowing your answers were actually considered, not filtered by a form.

2

Qualification

You wait while we review it against real criteria: capacity to follow through, current foundation, clarity of goals — not just whether you can pay. You walk away with an honest read on where you stand, before anything costs you money.

3

Blueprint

You receive a Financial Blueprint Assessment, built individually rather than pulled from a template. You walk away with a complete, honest picture of where your money actually stands — free either way.

4

Placement

You get a straight answer, never left vague: ready for a program now, or a clear picture of what to build first. You walk away with a specific next step at your stage, today — nobody gets sent away with nothing.

5

Education

You begin with education CHEW selects around your Blueprint, not a generic course everyone gets. You walk away understanding why each piece applies to your situation, not just that it exists.

6

Implementation

You do the actual follow-through — nobody else can do that part for you. CHEW provides the strategy, the education, and the accountability to keep you honest about it. You walk away with real progress, not a plan sitting in a folder.

7

Progress

You track action items and milestones as you go. CHEW keeps them visible in your CHEW Command Center instead of scattered across calls and notes. You walk away with a running record of what's actually been done.

8

Optimization

You keep living your life; CHEW refines strategy based on what's actually working for you, not the original plan for its own sake. You walk away with a system that gets sharper over time instead of going stale.

9

Capital Readiness

If funding is part of your goals, CHEW helps you prepare the documentation and positioning lenders and investors actually look for. You walk away ready for that conversation, not guessing at what they want to see.

10

Long-Term Advisory

CHEW measures success in years, not a single transaction. Membership continues the relationship after your program. You walk away with ongoing access to your advisor, not an engagement that ends the day you finish.

Where to start

Every stage starts the same way.

There's one entry point to all ten stages: a short assessment. If you have questions first, reach out through our contact form or email leroyt@joinchew.com directly.
