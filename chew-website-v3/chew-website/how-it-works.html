How It Works

Ten stages. One long-term relationship.

1

Application

You complete a short assessment and answer honestly — it's the only way we can tell whether we're the right fit.

2

Qualification

We review it against real criteria: capacity to follow through, current foundation, and clarity of goals — not just whether you can pay.

3

Blueprint

You receive a Financial Blueprint Assessment — a complete, honest picture of where your money actually stands.

4

Acceptance

You get a clear answer: a fit, a conditional fit, the waitlist, a referral elsewhere, or an invitation to revisit later.

5

Education

You begin with the education mapped to your specific roadmap — not generic content.

6

Implementation

You do the work. CHEW provides strategy and education; you provide the follow-through.

7

Progress

Your action items and milestones live in your CHEW Command Center, tracked as you go.

8

Optimization

As your systems mature, we refine strategy based on what's actually working for you.

9

Capital Readiness

If you're pursuing funding, we help you prepare the documentation and positioning lenders and investors actually look for.

10

Long-Term Advisory

CHEW measures success in years, not a single transaction. Membership continues the relationship after your program.

Where to start

Every stage starts the same way.

There's one entry point to all ten stages: a short assessment. If you have questions first, reach out through our contact form or email leroyt@joinchew.com directly.
