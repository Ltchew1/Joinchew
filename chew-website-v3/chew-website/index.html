The Real Problem

Broke means not enough coming in. Unorganized means plenty comes in — and leaves. Most people who feel behind aren't earning too little; they're running real income with no structure. That's not a flaw. It's a missing framework. CHEW builds the framework.

Most people don't avoid looking at their finances because they don't care. They avoid it because looking feels like proof that something's wrong with them — even when nothing is. There's a specific kind of quiet that comes with earning good money and still not knowing where it went by the end of the month. If that's familiar: there's nothing wrong with you. There's just no system yet.
