Programs

One system. Three levels of structure.

These aren't separate services — they're stages of the same system: clarity, a plan, and the accountability to follow through. Every applicant is matched to the level that fits where they are today.

◆
Level One — Organized

Infrastructure Program

Who it's for: individuals and entrepreneurs ready to build real structure around income that currently has none.

What's built: clarity on where your money goes, and a system built around your numbers — the foundation everything else stands on.

What's expected of you: participation, honesty, and follow-through on assigned action items.

◆
Level Two — Optimized

Executive Advisory

Who it's for: founders and individuals with more complex situations who want direct, higher-touch access to a strategist.

What's built: everything in the Infrastructure Program, plus a faster cadence and closer advisory for decisions that don't wait.

What's expected of you: the same accountability, at a pace matched to more complex decisions.

◆
Level Three — In Control

Membership

Who it's for: clients who've built the system and want to keep it running.

What's built: continued access to your advisor, strategy check-ins, and the education library — so structure stays a habit, not a memory.

What's expected of you: staying engaged with your systems, not just letting them run on autopilot.

What CHEW doesn't do

We won't call it something it isn't.

CHEW isn't credit repair, a guaranteed loan approval, or a promise of a specific outcome — and we won't pretend otherwise. Through CHEW Credit Lab, we show you how to review your own credit report and how to dispute items you don't recognize or didn't authorize — prepared, signed, and filed by you, directly with the bureaus, as your own right under federal law. This is education and tools, not credit repair: we don't file anything for you, we never touch accurate information, and no result is guaranteed. We're not a substitute for a licensed CPA, attorney, or registered investment advisor either — where your need falls into one of those lanes, we'll say so and point you to the right professional.
