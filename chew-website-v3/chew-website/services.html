What CHEW doesn't do

We won't call it something it isn't.

CHEW isn't credit repair, a guaranteed loan approval, or a promise of a specific outcome — and we won't pretend otherwise. Through CHEW Credit Lab, we show you how to review your own credit report and how to dispute items you don't recognize or didn't authorize — prepared, signed, and filed by you, directly with the bureaus, as your own right under federal law. This is education and tools, not credit repair: we don't file anything for you, we never touch accurate information, and no result is guaranteed. We're not a substitute for a licensed CPA, attorney, or registered investment advisor either — where your need falls into one of those lanes, we'll say so and point you to the right professional.
